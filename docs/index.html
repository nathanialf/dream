Reading the levels

Identified
bytes fall inside a region with a known class (config/regions.txt).
Extracted
bytes sit inside a named asset of a real kind (not unknown), split into its own asset file. Shown as the lighter fill and the smaller number below.
Round‑trips (matched)
the asset's kind is listed in config/roundtrip.txt: its editable form re-encodes to the exact ROM bytes. Shown as the solid fill and the larger number. stale/filler/unknown have nothing to round-trip, so matched = extracted for them.
Recomp
traced 65816/SPC700 routines listed in config/recomp.txt: reimplemented in C and passing the lockstep check against the ROM. This is the project's end target, shown in the headline above.
